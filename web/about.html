Gap Map

You have a gap between two classes. Gap Map lists the events on ASU’s Tempe campus that you can walk to in that gap and still reach your next class on time, and shows the coffee and food you pass on the way.

It is a student project for the WebGIS course. Open the app to try it.

Using it

Type where you are and where your next class is, as a building name or a code like MU or COOR, then the time class starts. You can also drag the A and B pins on the map. “More options” lets you change the day, how long you want to stay at an event, and the buffer you want before class.

Select an event to see the route and the places on the way. There is a link that opens the same walk in Google Maps. The map switches between a street map and satellite images; the satellite one shows buildings the street map is missing.

What “can make it” means

For each event on the chosen day, the walk from A to the event, the time you stay, the walk on to B, and the buffer have to fit between the time you are free and the time class starts. An event that is already running counts, using its real end time.

A coffee or food place is shown as “on the way” when going through its building adds at most 2 minutes of walking, fits in the time you have to spare, and is not known to be closed when you would get there.

How it is built

There is no server code. Python scripts do the heavy work ahead of time and save plain files. The page loads those files and does the check in the browser, with Leaflet for the map.

Everything is joined by the ASU building code (building_id), the same code students see on their schedule. Walking times between all 261 Tempe buildings are worked out beforehand from OpenStreetMap walkways, so ranking events is a lookup. The route on the map is searched in the browser for the one event you pick.

Data

SourceUsed for
ASU events feedEvents: title, time, place, poster. 1,000 event times (620 events), 25 October 2026 to 21 February 2027, from two downloads (25 October and 23 November). 20 to 22 November are missing.
NominatimTurning event addresses into points. 766 of the 1,000 were found.
ASU Facilities public map service (three layers)261 Tempe buildings with their ASU codes, 216 entrance points (surveyed in 2020) and 245 building outlines. Entrances and outlines were fetched on 14 November 2026.
OpenStreetMap walkwaysThe walking network and the walking times.
ASU Dining37 food and coffee places; 33 have weekly opening hours, which we checked by hand.

What is missing or approximate:

  • Tempe campus only. 496 of the 1,000 event times have no Tempe building: they are online, on other campuses or off campus, or could not be matched.
  • Most building entrances are estimated from ASU entrance points or building outlines, not checked by hand. We compared 10 routes with Google Maps and the walking times differ by about 1.2 minutes on average.
  • 18 dining places still have no building, so they are not shown. No stairs, no elevation, no waiting at crossings.

Where it stands

Working: event data and matching to buildings, walking times between all buildings, the Gap Map page with routes, coffee and food on the way, and the map and satellite switch. Not done yet: hand-checked entrances for the large buildings, and the 18 dining places that still have no building. Right now you enter one next class; a saved schedule with several classes is planned for after this course.

The event data is a download of what ASU lists. It has no events for 20 to 22 November, and the feed only lists upcoming events, so the data has to be refreshed from time to time.
